- Warm Up remains mandatory on each player's first available block.
- Machine remains mandatory for every hitter.

46-HITTER EXPECTATION
- 180 minutes / 12 minutes = 15 blocks.
- Opening consumes each on-time hitter's first block.
- 14 rotation blocks remain.
- Mandatory Front Toss capacity is 8 hitters in each non-Live rotation.
- 46 hitters require at least 6 non-Live rotations.
- Therefore the scheduler may attempt up to 8 Live rotations.
- 8 x 4 = 32 hitter Live opportunities.
- Best physical outcome under these rules is 14 hitters missing Live, before any pitcher/warm-up/arrival constraints reduce that capacity.
- Missing Live hitters should be eligible for the existing second-Front-Toss replacement path.

DEPLOYMENT RULE
Do not alter index.html or add a startup import. Once scheduler.mjs is patched and validated, deploy only scheduler.mjs plus a cache-bust of the existing worker import if the browser otherwise retains the old worker.
