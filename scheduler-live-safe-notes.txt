For 46 hitters in a 180-minute / 12-minute-block practice: 15 total blocks, 14 post-opening rotations. At 4 Live hitters per rotation, the physical maximum is 32 Live hitters, so at least 14 hitters must receive the approved second-Front-Toss replacement unless practice time or another physical rule changes.
